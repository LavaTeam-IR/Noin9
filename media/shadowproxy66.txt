• VLESS (Reality / XHTTP) • VMess • Trojan • Shadowsocks (R / 2022 / ShadowTLS / Restls) • Hysteria 1 & 2 • TUIC • WireGuard • Tailscale • OpenVPN • SSH • AnyTLS • Snell (v1 تا v5) • Masque • TrustTunnel • Gost-Relay • HTTP / SOCKS و چندین پروتکل دیگه.
